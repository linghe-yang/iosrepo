Select the desired update rate for the Spectrum Display.  The minimum is the default of 2.7 times per second, which is based on analyzing non-overlapping sound samples of 371 milliseconds each.  When a faster update rate is selected, the data analyzed is still 371 milliseconds long, but the buffers are overlapping to maintain the same resolution.  Select whatever rate looks good to you, keeping in mind that the fastest update rates do use battery power slightly faster than the default update rate.
