The legacy license is only for devices that do not have Wi-Fi or cannot use the forever license for some other reason.  All others should use the forever license since it is more flexible when changing devices.

When TuneLab is first installed on your device, it runs in free-trial mode, which occasionally displays a message instead of switching notes, but is otherwise the same as the licensed mode.

To purchase a license for your device and stop these free-trial messages, first verify that TuneLab runs properly on your device in free-trial mode.  If you do not see the boxes in the Phase Display move smoothly when a note is nearly in tune, then that device is not suitable for running TuneLab and should not be licensed.

Licensing using the legacy ID process starts by sending us the ID that uniquely identifies your device for TuneLab. This ID is the 20-digit ID shown at the bottom of the previous page. You can either copy down that ID and send it to us by e-mail, or you can tap on the "Send now by e-mail" button on the Licensing page and that ID will be automatically sent to us through whatever network connection your device may have (Wi-Fi or cell data) using the e-mail account that you had set up on your device.

Then go to our ordering page:

www.tunelab-world.com/orders

and place your order through PayPal with a major credit card or your PayPal account. When we receive your order we will match your order with your previously-sent device ID. If the device ID is valid then we will fill your order and send you the license number for your device. This license number is for one device only. But if you need to move your license to a different Android device you can contact us and we will help you do that. However, it is preferable to use the forever license because that license can be more easily transferred to a new device without having to contact us.

When you receive your license number, go to the Licensing page in TuneLab and enter the license number in the field provided.  The software will tell you immediately if the license number is correct for your device.
