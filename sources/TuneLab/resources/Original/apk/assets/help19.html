There are several ways to get more help and information about TuneLab Piano Tuner. There is a user's manual available as a free download from our support web page:

tunelab-world.com/atl

The manual is a PDF file that you might want to download for reference.

Also, there are several TuneLab discussion forums that you can join to share ideas, ask question, or just read what others are saying. If you would like to join such a group, follow the link on our website. Point your web browser at

tunelab-world.com/forum

Finally, if you have specific questions for us, or ideas for future improvements to TuneLab Piano Tuner, please e-mail us at:

info@tunelab-world.com
