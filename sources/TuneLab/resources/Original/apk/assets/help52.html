The "Alt Source" switch makes a minor change in the way audio data is delivered to TuneLab by the system.  In most Android devices this change has no effect on the operation of TuneLab.  But in at least one old device, turning the switch ON fixed a problem where a phantom frequency was being injected into the frequency spectrum.  At the time it was called the "B7 problem" because the phantom peak appeared when playing the notes B7 and C8.  The specific change is that when the switch is ON, the audio stream from the microphone will be requested with a source of:

MediaRecorder.AudioSource.VOICE_RECOGNITION

Otherwise, with the switch OFF, the audio stream will be requested with a source of:

MediaRecorder.AudioSource.MIC

These source settings may be implemented differently by different device manufacturers, therefore we maintain the option for you use either source format in case you think your device may have the "B7 problem" or some other audio anomaly.
