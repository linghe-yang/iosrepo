Dropbox Operations

Dropbox is the cloud storage solution provided by TuneLab for backing up and restoring your tuning files, temperament files, and report files generated by TuneLab.  You can sign up for a free account with Dropbox since the files involved in TuneLab are so small.

Initially, when you go to Dropbox OPs from the Files menu you will see three buttons, but two of them are grayed out.  The third button takes you to signing in to your Dropbox account.  You only need to do this once.  After you sign in, the other two boxes will become available.  They are Upload and Download.

When you Upload to Dropbox for the first time Dropbox will create a folder in your Dropbox cloud storage called "Apps/TuneLab Tuning Files".  In your first upload, all your tuning files will be uploaded to that folder, including the sample tuning files that came packaged with TuneLab.  After that, if you create more tuning files you can at any time choose to upload only the newer files to save time, and they will be added to your Dropbox.  There is more information on the uploading options on the upload page.  Once uploaded, you can access these files independently of the TuneLab app through the web interface that Dropbox provides.

At some point you may want to download files from Dropbox.  The typical case is when you change to a new device and install TuneLab fresh on that device.  You can then download all your tuning files to the new device.
