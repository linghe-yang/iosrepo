This page shows the current over-pull safety limits that are in effect. The purpose of safety limits is to override the over-pull calculations and ensure that the over-pull offset is never more than the specified amount. This is to prevent excessive over-pull offsets from causing strings to break. The limits are specified separately for wound strings and for plain strings. These limits can be set to anything from 10 cents to 115 cents in steps of 5 cents.

Whenever the safety limits are limiting the over-pull calculation, the safety limit value will be used and displayed in a redish background on the main tuning page.
