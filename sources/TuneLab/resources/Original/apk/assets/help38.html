On this page you can examine all the pre-measurements from the last over-pull, organized into sections of the scale determined by the over-pull settings in effect.  You can also delete any measurement by tapping on that measurement and confirming your intention to delete it.  If a measurement is deleted, TuneLab will extrapolate between the remaining measurements for the over-pull calculations so that it usually is not a problem to delete a questionable measurement.

If you want a permanent record of the pre-measurements you can send an e-mail containing all the pre-measurements to the address of your choice.  Just tap on the "more" button (with the three dots) and select "Send data by e-mail".
