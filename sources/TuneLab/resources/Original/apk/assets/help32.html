The over-pull factor is normally 1.0. That means the over-pull calculations (which are based on the pre-measurements) are used as is. Unless you have a good reason to change the over-pull factor, it is best to leave the over-pull factor set to 1.0. If you do change it with the selection spinner, the calculated over-pull for each note will be multiplied by the selected factor. For instance, if you change the factor to 0.50, all the over-pull amounts will be half as large as they would normally be. Since the need for over-pull is based on the interaction between strings through the flexibility of the plate and the soundboard, a piano with more or less flexibility in these two items might cause more or less interaction between strings, and therefore a change in the over-pull factor might be desired.
