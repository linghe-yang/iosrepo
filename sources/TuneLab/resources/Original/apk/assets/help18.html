By tapping on About this version in Settings you can see the version number and build date for this version of TuneLab. The Calibrated sample rate displayed here is the results of your calibration. The nominal sample rate is 22050.000. When you do a calibration, TuneLab determines and stores your device's actual sample rate, and uses that information to precisely calibrate TuneLab.

The button labelled "External memory file transfer" takes you to a page where you can copy tuning files to and from the external memory (like an SD card) on your device.  From that page you can also reinstall the tuning files that came with TuneLab and were initially installed the first time you ran TuneLab.
