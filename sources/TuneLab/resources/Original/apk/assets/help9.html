When you select an unequal temperament from the list of temperaments, the selected temperament is applied to the current tuning. These deviations from equal temperament are sometimes called "historical temperaments" because they are derived from tuning methods that were common in older musical periods, although some modern work has also been done in this area. An unequal temperament is defined by 12 offsets that are repeated in every octave. If a temperament is already applied to the current tuning, then there will be a "cancel current" button at the top of the list to allow you to simply remove the unequal temperament from the current tuning, returning the current tuning to equal temperament. (Note, this does not erase the temperament itself in your device. It merely removes it from the current tuning file.)

You can also rename or permanently delete any existing temperament from the list of temperaments in your device by tapping and holding on the item you want to rename or permanently delete.

After you select a temperament you will be prompted to choose whether you want the application of that temperament to be normalized or transposed. If you want no special treatment for the temperament then just tap on the Done button.

If you want to create a new temperament based on 12 offsets, then tap on the "new" button at the top of the list of temperaments.
